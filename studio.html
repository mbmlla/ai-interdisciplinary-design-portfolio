FutureSpace Studio Environment

A multidisciplinary studio concept designed for collaborative, client, and focused work.

Project brief

The studio was planned for 20 people with grouped desks, low partitions, private rooms for designer and client work, modular seating, natural light, biophilic details, and a large screen for sharing ideas.
